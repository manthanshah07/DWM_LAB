Data Warehousing and Mining (DWM) — Experiment 08¶

Experiment Title: Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering for Stock Market Profiling¶

Dataset: stock_market_data.csv (3 Years of Daily NSE Trading Data across 20 Major Indian Stocks)

1. Objectives & Problem Formulation¶

  • Objective: Perform Agglomerative Hierarchical Clustering on the stock market dataset, construct and interpret hierarchical dendrograms, and evaluate clustering quality across four linkage criteria (Single, Complete, Average, and Ward) using the Silhouette Score.
  • Adaptation Note:
    • Uses the multi-dimensional stock-level feature representation developed in Experiment 07 (Return: Avg_Daily_Return, Volatility: Annualized_Volatility, Avg_Intraday_Volatility, and Liquidity: log-transformed Avg_Volume and Total_Traded_Volume) to build bottom-up hierarchical dendrograms across all 20 NSE stocks.

3. Dataset Ingestion & Feature Engineering¶

  • Load stock_market_data.csv.
  • Convert Date to datetime and derive Intraday_Volatility as $\frac{\text{High\_Price} - \text{Low\_Price}}{\text{Open\_Price}} \times 100$.
  • Group by Ticker and Sector to compute the 5 core stock-level financial metrics (Avg_Daily_Return, Annualized_Volatility, Avg_Volume, Avg_Intraday_Volatility, Total_Traded_Volume).
df = pd.read_csv('stock_market_data.csv')
df['Date'] = pd.to_datetime(df['Date'])
df['Intraday_Volatility'] = (((df['High_Price'] - df['Low_Price']) / df['Open_Price']) * 100).round(2)

stock_df = (
    df.groupby(['Ticker', 'Sector'], as_index=False)
    .agg(
        Avg_Daily_Return=('Daily_Return', 'mean'),
        Annualized_Volatility=('Daily_Return', lambda x: x.std() * np.sqrt(252)),
        Avg_Volume=('Volume', 'mean'),
        Avg_Intraday_Volatility=('Intraday_Volatility', 'mean'),
        Total_Traded_Volume=('Volume', 'sum')
    )
    .sort_values('Ticker')
    .reset_index(drop=True)
)

stock_df['Avg_Daily_Return'] = stock_df['Avg_Daily_Return'].round(4)
stock_df['Annualized_Volatility'] = stock_df['Annualized_Volatility'].round(2)
stock_df['Avg_Volume'] = stock_df['Avg_Volume'].round(0).astype(int)
stock_df['Avg_Intraday_Volatility'] = stock_df['Avg_Intraday_Volatility'].round(2)

print(f"Stock Summary DataFrame Shape: {stock_df.shape}")

5. Hierarchical Linkage & Dendrogram Generation¶

Generate and plot hierarchical dendrograms for all four linkage criteria (metric='euclidean'):

  1. Single Linkage (method='single'): Minimum pairwise distance between clusters $\min \{d(a, b) : a \in A, b \in B\}$.
  2. Complete Linkage (method='complete'): Maximum pairwise distance between clusters $\max \{d(a, b) : a \in A, b \in B\}$.
  3. Average Linkage (method='average'): Mean pairwise distance across all pairs between clusters.
  4. Ward Linkage (method='ward'): Minimizes total within-cluster variance increase at each merge step.
# Plot 1: Dendrogram - Single Linkage
Z_single = linkage(X_scaled, method='single', metric='euclidean')
plt.figure(figsize=(12, 5))

6. Model Fitting across Linkage Methods ($n\_clusters = 3$)¶

Instantiate and fit AgglomerativeClustering(n_clusters=3) for single, complete, average, and ward linkages, and store the resulting cluster assignments in stock_df.

linkage_methods = ['single', 'complete', 'average', 'ward']
cluster_columns = {}

for method in linkage_methods:
    col_name = f"Cluster_{method.capitalize()}"
    agg_model = AgglomerativeClustering(n_clusters=3, linkage=method, metric='euclidean')
    stock_df[col_name] = agg_model.fit_predict(X_scaled)
    cluster_columns[method] = col_name

print("Agglomerative Clustering models fitted for all 4 linkage methods.")
display(stock_df[['Ticker', 'Sector'] + list(cluster_columns.values())])

7. Silhouette Score Evaluation & Comparison¶

Compute silhouette_score for each linkage method, display a sorted comparison DataFrame, and visualize the scores with a bar chart.

sil_records = []
for method, col_name in cluster_columns.items():
    score = silhouette_score(X_scaled, stock_df[col_name])
    sil_records.append({
        'Linkage_Method': method.capitalize(),
        'Silhouette_Score': round(score, 4)
    })

sil_df = pd.DataFrame(sil_records).sort_values(by='Silhouette_Score', ascending=False).reset_index(drop=True)
print("Silhouette Score Comparison across Linkage Methods (n_clusters = 3):")
display(sil_df)

plt.figure(figsize=(8, 5))
ax = sns.barplot(
    data=sil_df,
    x='Linkage_Method',
    y='Silhouette_Score',
    hue='Linkage_Method',
    palette='viridis',
    legend=False
)
for i, v in enumerate(sil_df['Silhouette_Score']):

11. Conclusion¶

Key Analytical Findings & Comparison with K-Means (Lab 7):¶

  1. Linkage Method Comparison & Chaining Effect:
    • Single Linkage exhibits the classic chaining phenomenon, merging 18 stocks into a single cluster while isolating M&M and ITC as singleton clusters (18 / 1 / 1), resulting in a negative Silhouette Score of $-0.1060$.
    • Average Linkage achieves the highest Silhouette Score of $0.4042$ (6 / 7 / 7 split, identical to K-Means in Lab 7), while Ward Linkage and Complete Linkage both achieve a strong Silhouette Score of $0.3922$ (7 / 7 / 6 split, shifting borderline HCLTECH into the higher-volatility cluster).
  2. Consistency Between Hierarchical Clustering (Lab 8) and K-Means (Lab 7):
    • At $K = 3$, Average and Ward Linkage recover the same three fundamental asset regimes discovered by K-Means (Lab 7):
      1. Low-Volatility, High-Liquidity Blue-Chip Core (7 stocks in Ward Cluster 1): HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK, INFY, ITC, KOTAKBANK, RELIANCE, SBIN.
      2. Moderate-Volatility, High-Denomination Defensive/Quality (6 stocks in Ward Cluster 2): BAJAJ-AUTO, BRITANNIA, HINDUNILVR, MARUTI, NESTLEIND, TCS.
      3. High-Volatility Cyclical & Energy PSU Growth (7 stocks in Ward Cluster 0): HCLTECH, M&M, NTPC, ONGC, POWERGRID, TATAMOTORS, WIPRO.
    • Beyond flat partitioning, the Dendrograms reveal fine-grained hierarchical sub-clusters (such as the tight pairing of Energy PSUs NTPC–POWERGRID, private banks ICICIBANK–KOTAKBANK–HDFCBANK, and defensive FMCGs HINDUNILVR–NESTLEIND).
  3. Readiness for Experiment 09: With both partitioning (K-Means) and hierarchical (Agglomerative) clustering completed, the market dataset is ready for Apriori Association Rule Mining (Experiment 09) to uncover cross-stock and cross-sector co-movement rules.
